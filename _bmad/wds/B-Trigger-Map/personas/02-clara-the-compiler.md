# Clara the Compiler - Primary Persona

> Primary target - Feeds the knowledge engine with dense research datasets, driving core system utilization and citation validation.

**Priority:** PRIMARY 🎓
**Role in Flywheel:** Clara's dense, high-volume document uploads test the boundaries of our knowledge extraction and graph connection engines. When she creates a highly polished, shareable atlas, she shares it with peers and advisors, introducing new organic users to the platform.
**Created:** 2026-06-08

---

## Profile Summary

Clara is a second-year PhD candidate in Bioinformatics who finds herself drowning in the sheer volume of literature she must read and synthesize. With hundreds of research PDFs, book chapters, and personal notes scattered across Zotero and various desktop folders, she experiences constant cognitive fatigue. She isn't looking for another chatbot to converse with; she needs a structured, visual knowledge hub that maps out the links between research papers, theories, and experimental results automatically.

By solving Clara's need for absolute factual accuracy, citation tracing, and zero-effort website creation, we establish the highest standard of trust for Instant Wiki.

## Current Situation

### Professional Reality

**The Daily Struggle:**
- **Information Fragment:** Reads 5-10 research papers per week, saving notes in disjointed text files.
- **Lost Context:** Often remembers a key concept (e.g., "variant effect prediction algorithms") but forgets which specific paper out of a hundred discussed it.
- **Advisor Updates:** Needs to present regular literature reviews to her advisor, requiring hours of manual slide assembly.
- **Cognitive Load:** Struggling to see the big picture connections across multiple distinct sub-fields.

**Skills & Tools:**
- **High Skills:** Python, Zotero, R, LaTeX, markdown note-taking.
- **Low Skills:** Frontend web development, database management, web hosting.
- **Learning Attitude:** Eager to use tools that save time, but highly skeptical of "AI magic" that lacks traceable sources.

**The Synthesis Gap:**
- Clara's core block is that Zotero acts as a static graveyard for PDFs, while Notion requires manual page creation and linking.
- She needs a system that does the heavy lifting of parsing, summarization, and connection mapping, but outputs a clean, publishable site.

## Driving Forces

### ✅ Top 3 Positive Drivers (What Clara Wants)

**1. Connection Discovery**
- Clara wants the AI to automatically identify and visually map out how concepts in separate PDFs overlap and relate (e.g., how "Paper A's methodology" relates to "Paper B's results").
- *Why it matters:* It saves weeks of manual mapping and uncovers research patterns she might have missed.
- *What success looks like:* A clean, interactive mini-graph showing nodes connecting bioinformatics papers.
- **Instant Wiki Promise:** The Knowledge Graph Preview and Graph Page dynamically render and connect extracted entities.

**2. Academic Impression**
- Clara wants to present a professional, structured website of her research to her advisor and lab peers.
- *Why it matters:* Establishes her authority and shows she has a deep, structured grasp of her field.
- *What success looks like:* A published `instant.wiki/clara/genomics-atlas` that looks like a premium digital textbook.
- **Instant Wiki Promise:** The public wiki output uses elegant serif Merriweather typography and a GitBook/Wikipedia layout.

**3. Zero-Configuration Publishing**
- Clara wants to turn a folder of PDFs into a clean site with a single drag-and-drop.
- *Why it matters:* She has no time or interest in learning Docusaurus, Astro, or configuring servers.
- *What success looks like:* Uploading 5 PDFs and getting a live site in 30 seconds.
- **Instant Wiki Promise:** The upload flow handles PDF parsing and generates pages, concepts, and relationships behind the scenes.

---

### ❌ Top 3 Negative Drivers (What Clara Fears)

**1. AI Hallucination & Academic Errors**
- Clara fears the AI making up claims or misrepresenting scientific data, which would ruin her credibility if shared.
- *Why this terrifies her:* Academic integrity is paramount; citing incorrect AI summaries to her advisor could derail her PhD.
- *What failure looks like:* A wiki page describing a bioinformatics tool using fabricated details.
- **Instant Wiki Answer:** Every Wiki Article page lists exact references and source document links, grounding every claim in Clara's uploaded files.

**2. Setup & Editing Time Sink**
- Clara fears that editing poorly formatted AI-generated pages will take longer than writing the documentation from scratch.
- *Why this terrifies her:* Time is her most scarce resource.
- *What failure looks like:* Cleaning up broken Markdown tables, double-bracket syntax errors, or disjointed bullet lists.
- **Instant Wiki Answer:** High-fidelity markdown parsers and a simple settings manager keep customization clean and structured.

**3. Tool Staleness & Organization Fatigue**
- Clara fears creating a wiki, uploading files once, and then having the portal become a static, abandoned page.
- *Why this terrifies her:* She has abandoned dozens of custom folders and Notion pages before.
- *What failure looks like:* A wiki that hasn't been updated in months because uploading new sources is tedious.
- **Instant Wiki Answer:** A simple drag-and-drop "Add Source" page that automatically regenerates and links new concepts into the existing wiki graph.
