# Citation Viewer Drawer [drw-001]

**Type:** Feedback / Modal
**Category:** Slide-out Panel
**Purpose:** Displays the context sentence and original document references on inline citation click

---

## Overview

The Citation Viewer Drawer slides out from the right side of the viewport when a reader clicks an inline citation badge (e.g. `[1]`). It displays the source PDF filename, page number, and highlights the exact sentence to establish absolute trust.

---

## States

**Required States:**
- **Open:** Panel visible on right, sliding in with 150ms animation. Backdrop layer dims page content.
- **Closed:** Panel hidden.
